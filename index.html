Rules of Whelmen

Basic Premise

You are an elemental mage dueling an opponent. In Whelmen, there is no separate life bar—your cards are your health and your mana.

Your total health is represented by the active cards remaining in your Hand and placed on your Board. If your cards run out, you are defeated!

The Turn Structure

At the start of your turn, you draw 1 card from the shared deck. Then, you may perform ONE of the following actions:

  • Play Mana: Move 1 card from your hand to the board as mana. You can only play 1 mana card per turn.
  • Cast a Spell: Select up to 3 mana cards on your board and consume them to cast a spell. Casting a spell consumes your active cards, reducing your overall health!
  • Pass: Skip playing mana or casting a spell to draw 1 additional card.

The Environmental Cycle

A predictable, elemental weather cycle rotates every single turn in this order:

Neutral ➔ Fire ➔ Earth ➔ Air ➔ Water ➔ Fire

  • Casting a spell matching the active Cycle element makes it Empowered (e.g., Firestorm does 7 damage instead of 4).
  • Casting a spell that is dominated by the active Cycle element makes it Weakened (e.g., a Fire spell when the Cycle is Water).

The Elemental Cycle Order

  • Dominating Advantage: Water beats Fire beats Earth beats Air beats Water.
  • Constructive Pairs: Fire and Air construct each other. Earth and Water construct each other.
  • Destructive Pairs: Fire and Water destruct each other. Earth and Air destruct each other.

Reactions (Defensive Window)

When your opponent casts an offensive spell, you will enter a brief Reaction Phase.

If you have active mana cards on your board, you can select them to cast a defensive reaction spell (like Pebble Shield or Mudslide). Reaction spells resolve before the attack hits, setting up shields to protect your precious cards!
