Rules of Whelmen

The Premise

You are an elemental mage dueling an opponent. In Whelmen, there is no separate life bar—your cards are your health and your mana.

Your total health is represented by the active cards remaining in your Hand and placed on your Board. If your cards run out, you are defeated!

The Structure

At the start of your turn, you draw 1 card from the shared deck. Then, you may perform BOTH of the following actions in any order:

  • Play Mana: Move 1 card from your hand to the board as mana. You can only play 1 mana card per turn.
  • Cast a Spell: Select up to 3 mana cards on your board and consume them to cast a spell. Casting a spell consumes your active cards, reducing your overall health!

Alternatively, you may Pass & Draw: Skip your actions to draw 1 additional card.

Overwhelmed

Your Maximum Hand Size is normally 8. If you exceed this limit, you must discard cards at the end of the turn. If you are forced to discard cards due to exceeding your hand size 2 turns in a row, you become Overwhelmed! Your Maximum Hand Size will decrease by 1. A player can become Overwhelmed any number of times.

The Cycle and Synergies

A predictable, elemental weather cycle rotates every single turn in this order:

Neutral ➔ Fire ➔ Earth ➔ Air ➔ Water ➔ Fire

Spells are categorized by how their component elements interact with each other and the active Cycle.

  • Constructive Spells: Contain complementary elements (Air/Fire or Water/Earth). They are empowered when their primary element matches the active Cycle.
  • Destructive Spells: Contain conflicting elements (e.g. Air/Water). They are empowered when their minority element matches the active Cycle.
  • Prestructive Spells: Perfectly balanced components (Neutral). They are empowered only when the active Cycle does not match ANY of the spell's components.

Reactions

When your opponent casts an offensive spell, you will enter a brief Reaction Phase.

If you have active mana cards on your board, you can select them to cast a defensive reaction spell (like Pebble Shield or Mudslide). Reaction spells resolve before the attack hits, setting up shields to protect your precious cards!
